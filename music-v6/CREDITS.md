# Music candidates v6

Round 6: JRPG-style battle and boss themes for the main Starvos fight, aiming for the 16-bit to PS1-era feel
(driving bass, rock/orchestral hybrid, synth strings and brass, heroic but dangerous). Every item is
Creative Commons 0 (public domain dedication); the license was verified on each item's own OpenGameArt page on
2026-10-30. Attribution is not required and is listed for reference.

None of these are covers or remixes of a commercial game. Tracks whose pages say they were inspired by a specific
Final Fantasy song were skipped (see below). Nothing from rounds 3 or 5 is repeated.

These were chosen from titles, descriptions and a rough tempo/loudness analysis (not by ear), so give each a
listen. None of the pages mention vocals, but no one has listened for voices yet. BPM numbers are automatic
estimates and may be off by half or double (mintodog's numbers come from the file names, so they are reliable).

Every file was level-matched to about -20 dB mean volume, the same as rounds 3 and 5, and encoded as 192 kbps
stereo mp3.
