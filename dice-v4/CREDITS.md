# Dice candidates v4

Candidates for N8 to compare: pick one D20 sound and one D6 sound (plus a pair, a handful, and optionally a shake).
Every item is Creative Commons 0 (public domain dedication). I checked the license on each item's own Freesound page on 2026-10-30.
All files are Freesound's HQ mp3 previews (real recordings, no synths). Attribution isn't required; it's listed for reference.
Edits: leading silence below -45 dB was removed, anything over 2 s was cut to 2 s with a 0.3 s fade-out, and quiet sources were raised to about a -1 dB peak so they compare fairly.
I picked these from titles, descriptions and tags, not by ear, so give each a listen. The ksigloch takes (the -a files) come from a single set where every die was recorded into the same pleather dice tray, so they match each other.

## Gaps

- Few real CC0 recordings of a single resin D20 on bare wood exist. d20-roll-a/b are into a tray, d20-roll-c is plastic on an unstated surface, d20-roll-d is a *metal* D20, and only d20-roll-e (0.84 s) says "wooden desk".
- d20-pair-c is a D12 + D20 rather than two D20s. d20-pair-d is a short bonus take.
- d6-roll-e is in a dice tower (more rattle), and d6-roll-c's recordist says it could be any die shape.
- d6-handful-b/c are three dice, short (0.7 to 0.8 s). The ksigloch handfuls (-a, -d) are the fullest.
- Bonus -d picks for d20-pair and d6-handful. I left out two dice shakes: a dice-bag rattle (0.5 s, noisy podcast audio) and cup shakes (per the brief).
- Not reused: Flem0527 "Dice Rolling on Table" and ksigloch "D6 - Single - 3", which N8 already heard.
