# Music candidates v7

Round 7: sweeping fantasy orchestral options for the Starvos battle, aiming for an "epic journey, trouble ahead"
feel (horns, strings, war drums, a perilous quest and looming danger, battle-ready but cinematic). All are
original works posted by their composers; none are covers of film scores. Nothing from rounds 3, 5 or 6 (or any
earlier round) is repeated.

Licenses were verified on each track's own OpenGameArt page on 2026-10-30:

- **starvos-r to starvos-w are CC0** (public domain dedication). Attribution is not required and is listed for reference.
- **Files ending in `-by` are CC-BY, not CC0.** They may be used freely, including commercially, but the game
  must credit the composer as shown below (for example in a credits screen or SOUND-CREDITS.md). No BY-SA, NC or
  ND tracks were included.

These were chosen from titles, descriptions and a rough tempo/loudness analysis (not by ear), so give each a
listen. None of the pages mention vocals; no one has listened for voices yet. BPM numbers are automatic estimates
and may be off by half or double.

Every file was level-matched to about -20 dB mean volume (same as rounds 3, 5 and 6) and encoded as 192 kbps
stereo mp3. Trimmed tracks get a 4 s fade-out.
