# Music candidates v5

Round 5: more "techno metal" tracks to play after Starvos Enraged (reference: "Project Mass" by Umplix), and more
dark epic boss music for the main Starvos fight (references: "Boss Battle Music" by SubspaceAudio and
"The Final Battle" by skrjablin). Every item is Creative Commons 0 (public domain dedication); the license was
verified on each item's own OpenGameArt page on 2026-10-30. Attribution is not required and is listed for reference.

These were chosen from titles, descriptions and a rough tempo/loudness analysis (not by ear), so give each a listen.
None of the pages mention vocals and the metal tracks are described as instrumental, but no one has listened for
voices yet. BPM numbers are automatic estimates and may be off by half or double.

Every file was level-matched to about -20 dB mean volume, the same as the current starvos-a, starvos-e and
starvos-enraged files, and encoded as 192 kbps stereo mp3.
