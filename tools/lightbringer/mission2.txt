“Please\! I don't understand. Lightbringer, what is happening? Who would…” Garthal stammers at you in shock before fading into unconsciousness.

You take care and arrange them into a more natural position before you race after Kilnor. A long corridor stretches ahead and you can see the abluza glimmer of the Life Crystal flashing off of the monster's metallic shoulder as it shambles into the waterfall chamber you teleported to from the comet. You activate the yemillow star sphere of strength to launch yourself at Kilnor, but right before you reach them, they plunge the spike into the crystal, releasing a concussive wave of dark energy that hurls you against the wall causing a cascade of rubble to crumble on top of you. Before you lose consciousness, the last thing you see is the light from the ucrystal funneling into a convulsing Kilnor and being replaced by the bleck fog that possessed the Hinon life crystal before it shattered.

The Blightbringer

You cough and hack up gritty sludge you inhaled from the dirt and debris that collapsed on you. When you open your eyes, they are burning and caked with the same grit. In fact your whole body is aching and heaving trying to clear your system of this gunk. Still, your limbs are pinned and panic and fear start to tingle deep inside you as you struggle to move and breathe. The fear is getting stronger, which causes a response from it's counterpart, Love. A shiver of Love vibrates through you and activates your star sphere of strength to flex yourself free. A sharp pain causes the power to dissipate as you emerge from the rubble. No matter how much strength you have, it does not fix broken bones. Your right wrist and ankle are very tender and not functioning properly, but you are able to make it to a standing position and rub the grit and tears from your eyes.
