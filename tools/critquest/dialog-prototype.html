    How this guides the player

    • Tap or press Space to finish a line; pick answers by tapping or pressing 1 to 4.
    • Your first answers to Bruxanna and Wexler quietly set your Style (Serious, Silly, Shy or Smug). When you build your new self, you can keep that Style or change it.
    • After that, every talk adds one bonus answer in your Style, marked with its tag.
    • Style answers work on some beings and not others. When one works, that being warms to you, and you may get a clue, a better price or a favor later. Try Restart with each Style to compare.
    • Some answers show a check, like Charm 12. You roll D20 + Charm, and the result changes the reply, never blocks the story.
    • Important clues go into the Journal, and the scene ends with a quest pop-up so the player always knows what's next.
