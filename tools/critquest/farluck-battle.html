Light Keeper Sparring

Crit Quest hex prototype 2 · three level 3 Heroes against three, with Combos

Build three Light Keepers for a sparring bout on the Castle Comet. Each Hero follows one Focus of their Role and picks 1 of 2 abilities at level 1 and again at level 3. Each turn is one free Move plus two actions; you may Attack twice, but only one ability (or Combo) a round. Heroes with different Focuses can join their abilities into Combos.
