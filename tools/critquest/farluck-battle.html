How to play

Each turn you get one free Move plus two actions. You may Attack twice, but only one ability (or Combo) a round.

Press an action in the bar, then tap a lit hex or a circled target. Hover a button or a Hero for a reminder.

Your Crit Dice roll on the field: a D20 to hit (beat the target's Defense), then the damage dice. A natural 20 is a crit and doubles the damage dice. A natural 1 misses and brings a Calamity.

Trees give cover, hills give +1 to hit from above, rocks block sight. The Chronicle keeps every roll.
