      How to play

      • Pick an action on the bar (Walk, Look, Use, Talk, Take), then tap something in the scene. Tap the ground to walk there.
      • With Walk on, tapping something does the obvious thing: look at it, or go that way.
      • What your Light Keeper notices and says appears above their head.
      • Talking opens a window with answers. Your Style adds one bonus answer, and some people warm to it.
      • Your Ready slots hold food or potions for the road. Open the Pack to see what you've found and your Journal.
      • The Return Shard takes you home to the Castle Comet at any time, with your Pack. Once you help someone, their hearth becomes a free way home.
      • When a fight starts, the game switches to the hex battle map.

      This is a mockup: the art is simple placeholder shapes, and the frame color and corner marks change with your Role.
